INCOG PLOTLINE — persistent AI voiceover setup

1. Replace src/App.jsx and src/App.css with the included files.
2. In Supabase, open SQL Editor, paste/run supabase-voiceover-setup.sql once.
3. Open Creator Studio > Create story (or Edit story).
4. Generate narration with ElevenLabs or another AI voice service and download the audio file to your computer.
5. Use “Upload AI voiceover” and select the downloaded audio (MP3/WAV/M4A etc., up to 50 MB).
6. Wait for the upload success notice, then publish the story or click the save/update action. The uploaded URL is stored on the story.
7. Readers open the story and use the “Story narration” audio player to play it.

Important:
- Uploading is available in Creator Studio for the signed-in administrator, not as a reader-only button.
- The SQL migration adds the voiceover_url column and configures a public-read storage bucket with admin-only upload/update/delete rules.
- If the SQL reports that public.is_current_user_admin() does not exist, stop and tell the app maintainer; do not remove the admin-only policy.
- This uses an audio file exported from ElevenLabs/another AI voice tool. It does not call ElevenLabs or generate voices itself.
